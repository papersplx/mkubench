mkubench

Dataset v1.0 — historical

An automated LLM benchmark modeled after MMLU/MMLU-Pro, designed to evaluate large language models on their understanding of the classified history, testimonies, and technological evolution of government-sponsored mind-control, behavioral modification, and neuro-weaponry programs.

Historical results — dataset v1.0

These runs were scored on dataset v1.0. Dataset v2.0 corrected the Q37 answer key and hardened the Q31-Q40 distractors, so a v1.0 score is not comparable with a v2.0 score — not even for the same model. This page is kept for reference and continuity only.
